# Powering LEDs 

Powering your LEDs correctly is FUNDAMENTAL! 

Incorrect powering can cause overheat, wire melting, fire and electrical hazards. 

---
### Calculating Power

Before selecting a power source, you must calculate how much power your LED project requires.
This depends on:

- The type of LED

- Voltage

- Current

- Number of LEDs in use

---
### Power Supply or LED driver?

LED drivers are the ideal power source for LED projects. However, at hackSpace we advise using common power supplies for prototypes.

---
### Power Supply

Suitable for general equipment including LED strips, single LEDs, LED rings, LED bulbs, COB-LED strips.

NOT SUITABLE for COB bare chips or filaments. 

---
### LED driver

Mandatory use for COB lights such as COB bare chips and filaments.
